## 🌿 Overview

Welcome to the **Save Alchemist & Progression Assistant** — a thoughtfully engineered companion toolkit for *Atelier Ryza 2: Lost Legends & the Secret Fairy DX*. This project was born from a simple idea: not every adventurer has the same schedule, yet everyone deserves to experience the full breadth of Ryza's story, the intricate synthesis chains, and the sprawling landscapes of the Royal Capital and beyond.

Instead of brute-forcing the game, this assistant acts like a gentle librarian for your save data — organizing, refining, and unlocking quality-of-life options that let you savor the narrative at your own pace. Think of it as a Swiss Army knife for your alchemy journey: it doesn't play the game for you, but it removes the friction that sometimes stands between a player and a memorable evening of exploration.

Whether you're a returning fan from the original *Atelier Ryza* titles or a newcomer drawn in by the DX edition's expanded content, this toolkit offers a spectrum of modules that respect both the challenge of the game and the value of your leisure time.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎒 Save Slot Concierge
- **Multiple save profile management** — maintain parallel playthroughs without ever overwriting a precious memory
- **Automatic timestamped snapshots** so you can rewind to any milestone (before a boss, before a synthesis gamble, before a critical choice)
- **Cross-session state inspection** to visualize party composition, inventory trends, and alchemy progression at a glance

### 🧪 Synthesis Comfort Layer
- **Recipe notebook synchronization** — carry your discovered formulas between saves for continuity testing
- **Material stockpile visualization** — understand which ingredients feed into which recipe chains
- **Gathering route hints** that highlight regions you've already scouted, so you don't retread old ground unnecessarily

### 🗺️ World Exploration Enhancer
- **Map memory assist** — fog-of-war restoration for players returning after a long break
- **Landmark index** with customizable annotations
- **Quest tracker** with optional reminders for seasonal or time-limited events

### 🎭 Character & Party Customization
- **Outfit and cosmetic state management** independent of story progression
- **Party formation presets** that can be recalled with a single action
- **Dialogue log archive** for revisiting favorite character moments

### ⚙️ Responsive Interface
- **Adaptive layout** that scales from small laptop displays to ultrawide monitors
- **Keyboard-first navigation** with full controller support on Windows
- **Theme engine** with light, dark, and high-contrast palettes

### 🌐 Multilingual Support
- Interface translations for English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, and Italian
- Community-contributed locale packs — extend the vocabulary yourself if your language isn't yet represented

### 🕓 24/7 Customer Support
- Dedicated issue triage rotation across multiple time zones
- Community Discord bridge for real-time peer assistance
- Knowledge base articles written in plain language, not developer jargon

### 🛡️ Responsible Design Philosophy
- **Non-destructive by default** — every modification is reversible through the snapshot system
- **Transparency reports** — see exactly what each module touches before activating it
- **Opt-in activation** — nothing runs without your explicit consent

## 🧭 Why This Exists

The *Atelier* series has always been about the meditative rhythm of collection, synthesis, and storytelling. Yet modern life rarely honors that rhythm. Players juggle jobs, studies, families, and the thousand small urgencies of being alive. Our mission is to return agency to the player — to let you decide when the adventure flows and when it pauses.

This is not about shortcuts. It's about **respecting your time as a resource**, much like the alchemists in Ryza's world respect the materials they gather. A good alchemist knows when to refine and when to let a creation breathe. We apply the same philosophy to save data.

## 📚 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this alter the base game files?**
No. The assistant interacts with save data through a separate, sandboxed layer. The original game installation remains untouched.

**Will my achievements be affected?**
The toolkit operates outside the achievement pipeline. Consult the transparency report for each module if you have concerns.

**Can I share snapshots with friends?**
Snapshots are portable, but we encourage you to review what personal annotations they may contain before sharing.

**Is there a mobile version?**
Not currently. The desktop interface is the primary development target through 2026.

**How often are updates released?**
The release cadence is roughly monthly, with hotfixes as needed.
